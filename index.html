Privacy Policy for TigerME

Effective Date: May 1, 2025

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

TigerME ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the TigerME mobile application (“App”).

By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use TigerME:

  • Personal Information: This includes your name, email address, GPA, test scores, extracurricular activities, target schools, and other college admissions-related data you provide.
  • Demographic Information: We may collect optional data such as your gender, race/ethnicity, or parents’ education level to provide more personalized admissions guidance.
  • Subscription Data: We collect and track subscription status (monthly or yearly) via RevenueCat, a secure third-party service provider.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymized usage statistics to improve the app's performance and features.
  • Profile Photo: If you choose to add one, the photo you upload.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Generate personalized college admissions recommendations
  • Provide access to premium features based on your subscription
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Respond to support requests and user inquiries
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Subscription & Payments

TigerME offers two subscription options:

  • Monthly Subscription: $4.99 per month
  • Yearly Subscription: $44.99 per year

Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store and billed via your Apple ID account. You may cancel or modify your subscription at any time via your iOS device settings.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure services (including Firebase and RevenueCat) to store and protect your data. While no method of transmission is 100% secure, we work hard to ensure your information is safe.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You may request to delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at support@tigermeapp.com or using the in-app “Delete My Account” feature.

Profile photos from earlier versions of the app

On or after December 1, 2026, we will remove profile photos that were added with earlier versions of our iPhone app, because we cannot reliably connect those photos to their accounts, which we need to do to delete a photo when its account is deleted. Only the profile photo is affected; the rest of the account stays the same. Removed photos can be recovered by us for 7 days after removal to correct mistakes. We are also emailing affected accounts that have a verified sign-in email address. To add a new photo afterwards, you will need the updated iPhone app, once available.

6. Children’s Privacy

TigerME is intended for use by high school students and requires parental consent where applicable. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 without appropriate legal consent.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. You will be notified of any material changes via in-app notifications or updates to this page.
