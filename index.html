Privacy Policy for TigerME

Effective Date: May 1, 2025

Last Updated: PUBLICATION_DATE

TigerME ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the TigerME mobile application (“App”).

By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use TigerME:

  • Personal Information: This includes your name, email address, GPA, test scores, extracurricular activities, target schools, and other college admissions-related data you provide.
  • Optional Profile Details: If you choose to provide them: your gender, race/ethnicity, nationality, household income range, intended major and minor, occupation, and your parents’ education and occupation. They are stored with your profile.
  • Subscription Data: We collect and track subscription status (monthly or yearly) via RevenueCat, a secure third-party service provider.
  • Usage and Device Data: The screens you open and actions you take in the app (for example, starting sign-up or redeeming a Student Code), together with identifiers for your app installation and device (an ID the app generates, Apple’s identifier for vendor and analytics instance IDs), your device model and operating system version. We use this to understand and improve the app. The app does not attach your name or email address to this data.
  • Crash and Diagnostic Data: Technical information about app crashes and errors, used to keep the app working.
  • School Search: The text you type when searching for your school, which is sent to Google Places to suggest matching schools.
  • Approximate Location: Our analytics provider estimates a general location, such as your country or region, from your IP address. The current version of the app does not ask for your device’s location. If you allowed location access in an earlier version, that permission stays in your iPhone settings and the Google Places service used for school search may receive your device’s location; you can turn it off in Settings > TigerME > Location.
  • Profile Photo: If you choose to add one, the photo you upload.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Generate personalized college admissions recommendations
  • Provide access to premium features based on your subscription
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Respond to support requests and user inquiries
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We do not use it for third-party advertising and do not share it with data brokers.

Service providers

We share information only with service providers that run parts of TigerME for us:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: sign-in, data and photo storage, the server that prepares your analyses, app analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) and app configuration.
  • OpenAI: when you request an analysis or essay help, our server sends your academic profile (GPA, SAT or ACT score, AP scores, activities, leadership roles, community service, awards, high school, year in school and target school) and, for essay help, your journal entries to OpenAI to generate the result. We do not send your name, email address or optional profile details.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status and purchase history, linked to your account ID.
  • Segment (Twilio): usage and device data described above, for analytics.
  • Google Places (Google Maps Platform): the school search text described above.
  • Apple: App Store payments. We never see your payment details.

3. Subscription & Payments

TigerME offers two subscription options:

  • Monthly Subscription: $4.99 per month
  • Yearly Subscription: $44.99 per year

Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store and billed via your Apple ID account. You may cancel or modify your subscription at any time via your iOS device settings.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure services (including Firebase and RevenueCat) to store and protect your data. While no method of transmission is 100% secure, we work hard to ensure your information is safe.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You may request to delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at support@tigermeapp.com or using the in-app “Delete My Account” feature.

Profile photos from earlier versions of the app

On November 18, 2026, we removed the profile photos that had been added with earlier versions of our iPhone app, because we cannot reliably connect those photos to their accounts, which we need to do to delete a photo when its account is deleted. Only the profile photo was affected; the rest of the account stayed the same. Removed photos can be recovered by us until November 25, 2026 (16:19 UTC) to correct mistakes. We had also emailed affected accounts that have a verified sign-in email address. To add a new photo, you will need the updated iPhone app, once available.

Older profile photos added on the website

On November 18, 2026, we removed the profile photos that had been added on the TigerMe website on or before November 16, 2026 at 21:35:33 UTC, including photos a parent added when creating a student’s account, because we cannot confirm which account added them, which we need to do to delete a photo when its account is deleted. Photos added after that time, and photos that our systems can confirm were added by the account itself, were not affected. Only the profile photo was affected; the rest of the account stayed the same. Removed photos can be recovered by us until November 25, 2026 (16:19 UTC) to correct mistakes. We email affected accounts only at a verified sign-in email address; sending an email does not confirm that it was delivered or read. To add a new photo, use the Profile screen of the updated iPhone app, once that version is available; the TigerMe website adds a photo only when an account is created.

6. Children’s Privacy

TigerME is intended for use by high school students and requires parental consent where applicable. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 without appropriate legal consent.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. You will be notified of any material changes via in-app notifications or updates to this page.
